# Catalog image sources — OBSIDIAN COMPUTE

Retrieved 2026-11-08 from Wikimedia Commons. The 60 catalog products are fictional demo configurations under the demonstration labels Obsidian, Forma and Northstar. Specifications and VND prices are sample data, not manufacturer offers. Photos are category illustrations reused across products and are not represented as exact model/configuration photos.

18 distinct source photographs are stored locally as WebP files. Each product has three different photos; these are not crops of one image. Images were resized proportionally (up to 1200 px) and converted to WebP. Original composition is retained. ## Verification

- 60 products, six categories with ten products each; three demo brands.
- 120 unique SKUs; two independent stock/price variants for each product.
- Six description lines and three valid local image URLs for every product.
- All 18 images decode successfully, have unique content hashes, and preserve source license metadata in `source/media/catalog/credits.json`.
- Photo review confirmed visible computer hardware appropriate to its category.
- Some photographs show third-party trademarks; those marks identify the illustrated hardware and do not imply sponsorship.
- For production use, replace illustrative demo photographs/specifications with accurate photos and verified specifications of actual merchandise.
